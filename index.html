Within · The Human Skull
An Interactive 3D Disarticulation Atlas

This interactive atlas presents the 22 bones of the human skull in an anatomically authentic Beauchene preparation. Every mesh is derived directly from the **BodyParts3D** dataset created by the Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS) in Japan, mapped to Foundational Model of Anatomy (FMA) identifiers.

The Beauchene Disarticulation Model

In classical anatomy, a Beauchene skull preserves the natural orientation and distance of each bone as if expanded outward from the central sphenoid core. This allows students, surgeons, and orthodontists to inspect internal articular facets, the sella turcica, pterygopalatine fossa, and cranial foramina without loss of spatial context.

Dataset Details

  • Total Meshes: 22 individually segmented bones (Neurocranium & Viscerocranium).
  • Coordinate Alignment: Native shared coordinate frame (sub-millimeter bilateral symmetry).
  • Origin Anchor: Sphenoid bone centroid at $[0, 0, 0]$.
  • Source: BodyParts3D 4.0 (CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan).
